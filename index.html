Official overview ↗ Official rules ↗

Objective

What we are optimizing

Official targetFinish with more bank than the opponent bot

Every episode is head-to-head. Unsold crops, animals, and shed inventory do not count.

OfficialSkill rating

Built from head-to-head episode outcomes.

DiagnosticFinal bank

Shows whether the farm economy is improving.

ReliabilityRuntime failures

Errors, timeouts, and invalid actions.

Environment

Fixed variables

The agent observes these rules; it cannot change them.

Match2 players · 720 turns

30 days with 24 turns each.

Starting state3,000 coins · 1 quadrant

Northwest 5 × 5 begins unlocked.

Board10 × 10 tiles

Three more quadrants cost 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000.

Storage100 shed items

Overflow at day-end is discarded.

Market limit10 orders per turn

Both players affect shared prices.

Basic needs2 missed days

Unwatered plants become weeds; unfed animals escape.

Labor cost1, 1, 2, 3, 5…

Daily hires follow Fibonacci pricing and reset each day.

World noise0.5% weed chance

Shops unlock every 3 days and consume stock every 4 turns.

Policy

Current decision tree

Lower number wins when tasks compete.

  1. 01
    Is value ready?

    Harvest crops or animal output before it decays or caps.

    HARVEST
  2. 02
    Is daily survival work due?

    Water plants, collect feed, and feed cattle.

    WATER · PICKUP · FEED
  3. 03
    Can an animal earn a bonus?

    Care after feeding to increase the next production event.

    CARE
  4. 04
    Has the cattle phase started?

    Build four pastures and place purchased cows.

    BUILD · PLACE
  5. 05
    Is a planned crop tile empty?

    Plant wheat plus melons before day 13; strawberries after.

    PLANT
  6. 06
    No local task?

    Move toward the next target; after hour 20, return carried output to the shed.

    MOVE · DROP · PASS

Market and phase gates

Always

Sell completed output already in the shed.

Hour 00

Hire six temporary hands for the day.

Days 00–12

Target 10 melons + 5 wheat plots.

Day 11+

Target 4 cows and keep at least 8 wheat feed in reserve.

Day 13+

Target 15 strawberries; buy the first expansion when bank ≥ 2,200.

Days 27–29

No gate yet. The agent continues reinvesting—this is the known exit defect.

Agent chooses

Portfolio, worker count, routing, crop timing, land purchases, market orders, and when to stop reinvesting.

Agent observes

Both farms, shared prices, town demand, current day/hour, its private seeds, shed, and carried inventory.

Agent cannot control

Episode seed, opponent actions, shop unlock sequence, weed rolls, rule constants, and the 720-turn deadline.
